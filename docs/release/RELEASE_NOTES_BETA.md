# PODX closed beta release notes — version placeholder

PODX helps buyers discover and compare products at nearby shops and gives sellers/admins local-commerce demo workspaces. This beta includes discovery, watchlists, alerts, requests, shop following, seller inventory/insights, admin workflows, privacy controls, offline mock behavior, and English/Telugu localization infrastructure.

OTP, payment, notifications, cloud sync, verification, monitoring upload, and legal services are simulations or placeholders. Do not use PODX for a real payment, identity decision, emergency, or legally binding action. Data may remain only on the device and demo data may reset. Do not enter secrets or sensitive personal information.

Use **Profile → Beta feedback** to save a bug, feature, UI, search, price, seller, performance, or other report locally. Reports are not automatically transmitted; follow the beta coordinator's approved process. Known limitations are maintained in the Beta Readiness Report.
